2. Confirm Minimum Pantry and Medium Pantry are unchanged.
3. Select Fully Stocked Pantry and confirm the 12 numbered sections.
4. Open a section and then open or close an individual type group.
5. Confirm **Expand** opens all sections and types; **Collapse** closes all of them.
6. Mark products In Stock, Low, and Out and confirm they continue to feed inventory/restock behavior.
7. Add a manual product, click **Clear**, and confirm the product remains while its check/status resets.
