# v84.30 Pantry Grouping and Freezer Order

## Freezer order

Complete Meals and Individual Recipes are sorted inside each cuisine accordion by their displayed code using numeric comparison. This keeps MEAL-2 before MEAL-10 and applies the same predictable ordering to individual recipe codes.

## Pantry order

Pantry accordion contents now use two explicit columns. The alphabetized product-type sequence fills the first column before continuing at the top of the second column. Items are alphabetized within each type.

## Product-type groups

Related Pantry products now remain together under compact headings. The grouping covers:

- Broths & Stocks, Soups, Beans, Meat & Seafood, Sauces, and Vegetables
- Barbecue, Asian, and Hot Sauces; Condiments; Sweeteners
- Baking Basics, Cooking Oils, Shelf-Stable Dairy, and Vinegars
- Breads & Crackers, Pasta & Noodles, Rice & Grains, and Tortillas
- Herbs & Spices, Salt & Pepper, and Seasoning Blends
- Baking Ingredients, Breakfast & Snacks, Mixes & Fillings, and Protein & Supplements
- Bags, Containers, Labels & Marking, and Wraps & Liners

Pantry level markers now use the compact **L1**, **L2**, and **L3** format.

## Data safety

These are display-order and grouping changes only. Pantry keys, Freezer record identifiers, saved quantities, notes, and status values remain unchanged.
