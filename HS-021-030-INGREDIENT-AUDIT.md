# HS-021–HS-030 Ingredient Audit

Approved September 6, 2026. Ingredient Standard version 1.36.

## Scope

- 10 Holiday Special recipes
- 77 ingredient rows restructured from manifest-authored display lines
- All 30 Holiday Special recipes and all 240 Holiday ingredient rows are now standardized
- Original source text retained on every ingredient
- Recipe IDs, cards, hero images, directions, servings, and designs preserved

## Applied standards

- Quantities and units were extracted from all 77 source lines.
- Cod retains four six-ounce fillets with a 24-ounce shopping total.
- Prime rib retains its explicit 5–6-pound range.
- Broccolini, prosciutto, puff pastry, eggs, and egg yolks retain practical natural counts.
- Matzo meal or flour remains an acceptable substitution group.
- Measured Parmesan and butter receive practical shopping equivalents.

## Retained review flags

- Three sliced onions omit sizes needed for a defensible cup conversion.
- Vegetable oil for frying has no source quantity.

No uncertain quantity was invented.
