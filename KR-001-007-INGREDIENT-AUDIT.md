# KR-001–KR-007 Ingredient Audit

Finalized September 5, 2026 from the cumulative v96.26 base.

## Applied standards

- Added all 7 Kolache recipes and 37 ingredient rows to the five-part ingredient model.
- Standardized refrigerated crescent roll dough to a cooking quantity of 2 cans with a practical shopping equivalent of two 8-ounce cans.
- Retained sausage and boudin weights, natural counts for eggs and small prepared meats, and cup measures for prepared ingredients.
- Separated preparation and purpose wording from canonical ingredient identity.
- Preserved “to taste” seasoning judgments without inventing quantities.
- Preserved the optional honey-mustard-or-favorite-sauce choice while excluding it from automatic Shopping Lists.

## Approved source-measure exceptions

- KR-002 diced cooked ham remains 1 cup because the source provides no reliable weight conversion.
- KR-003 bacon remains 6 slices because cooked slice weight varies and the source provides no package weight.

## Review status

All approved Kolache rules have been applied. The two source-measure exceptions are intentional and documented, not unresolved guesses.

Recipe IDs, approved images, recipe-card designs, directions, and unrelated functionality remain unchanged.
