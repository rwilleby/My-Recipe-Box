## Conservative exclusions

## Quantity handling

- Raw chicken ounce and pound requirements convert to pounds before consolidation.
- Cooked chicken requirements consolidate in ounces.
- Milk teaspoons, tablespoons, and cups convert to cups.
- Common fraction display now includes ⅜ and ⅝.
