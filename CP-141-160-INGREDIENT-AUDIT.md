# CP-141–CP-160 Ingredient Audit

- Category: Crock Pot Meals
- Recipes audited: 20
- Ingredient rows audited: 151
- Ingredient standard: 1.30
- Transcription corrections: 29
- Ingredient-row review flags retained: 71

## Approved treatments

- Meat and sausage identities and preparations remain intact; six thick pork chops remain a whole-item count because total weight is absent.
- Prepared carrots and diced apples use cup measures with approximate whole-produce equivalents. Uncertain cabbage-head and sliced-potato yields remain in their source units.
- Cheese uses practical ounce equivalents, eggs and tortillas use natural counts, and half-cup butter uses a one-stick equivalent.
- Dry rice state is explicit. Water or milk remains a choose-one substitution, and included cinnamon-roll icing is not a separate purchase.
- Generic peppers, beans, soup, and seasoning remain generic rather than being assigned invented varieties.

## Review flags

Fourteen package rows omit sizes, fifty-four rows omit usable quantities, one pork-chop row omits total weight, and two prepared-produce rows lack the size information needed for defensible cup conversions. Source information is retained for all 71 rows.
