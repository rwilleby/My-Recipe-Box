# Robert's Recipe Box v83.11 Planning and Shopping Report

## Weekend Bulk Plan

The food-source selector now follows the requested order:

1. Individual Recipes
2. Complete Meals
3. Diet Meals

Diet Meals displays only recipes in the DM category. These entries retain the existing recipe preview, add-to-plan, packaging, label, production, and inventory handoff behavior.

## Shopping List

Shopping List now has two views:

- **Consolidated List** keeps the existing aisle-based list with combined quantities.
- **By Meal / Component** shows every planned meal occurrence separately and lists its individual ingredients or prepared components. Refrigerator and freezer restock needs appear as their own groups.

The new **Clear & Start Over** action requires confirmation. It clears the Weekly Meal Planner source, shopping checks, shopping comments, and prepared-component decisions. Pantry, refrigerator, freezer, and prepared inventory are deliberately preserved; inventory-derived restock needs may therefore remain visible.
