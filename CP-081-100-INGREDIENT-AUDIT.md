## Approved treatments

- Meat and poultry weights remain accurate for cooking and consolidation.
- Prepared produce, cheese, bacon, rice, pasta, beans, and browned or cubed proteins separate preparation from identity.
- Generic broth and unspecified beans stay generic.
- Rice and pasta cooked separately retain dry shopping quantities.
- Missing quantities and can sizes remain visible and flagged instead of being guessed.

## Review flags

Fourteen canned-ingredient rows omit package sizes and twenty-two ingredient rows omit usable quantities. Source information is retained for all 36 rows. The proposal's stated total of 15 package rows was an arithmetic error; its itemized list contains the 14 rows implemented here.
