# My Recipe Box v99.16

## Standardized browsing control strips

Applied the approved Complete Dinners control-strip treatment to:

- Healthy Dinners
- Crock Pot Meals
- Recipe Library
- Vegan Recipe Library
- Your Favorites

## Page controls

- **Healthy Dinners:** Search, All, Beef, Chicken, Pasta, Seafood, Meatless, Vegan.
- **Crock Pot Meals:** Search, All, Chicken/Turkey, Beef, Pork/Ham, Sausage, Soups/Stews, More.
- **Recipe Library:** Search, All, Favorites and the most-used cuisines, with remaining recipe groups under More.
- **Vegan Recipe Library:** Search, All and the most-used vegan groups, with remaining groups under More.
- **Your Favorites:** Search, All, Recipes, Complete Dinners, Build-A-Meals.

The former Vegan Recipe Library filter toolbar was removed. Existing favorite-first and alphabetical sorting behavior remains unchanged.
