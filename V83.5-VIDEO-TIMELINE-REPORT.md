# Robert's Recipe Box v83.5 Video Timeline Report

## Starting images

Every Video Library entry now uses a dedicated 960×540 WebP poster extracted from the actual video footage. The posters show Robert or the real scene at the beginning of each video, fill the 16:9 player area, and replace the wide large-hero images that created black bars.

These posters belong specifically to the Video Library. Existing large-hero video behavior elsewhere on the site remains unchanged.

## Three-column layout

- Three columns on desktop and iPad landscape widths above 900 pixels
- Two columns between 761 and 900 pixels
- One column at 760 pixels and below

The cards use slightly smaller titles and compact timeline labels so three columns remain readable without changing the video aspect ratio.
