## Bread Machine registry

The repository contained 10 completed BR cards but no active BR recipe registry. v84.11 activates those cards as the Bread Machine category.

- BR-001, BR-002, BR-003, and BR-005 through BR-011 are active.
- BR-004 does not exist and was not invented.
- BR-011a is an alternate image for BR-011 and does not become a second recipe.
- A printed “4 cups flour total” summary is not treated as an additional purchase when the same card separately lists the component bread and all-purpose or whole-wheat flours.

## Ingredient handling

- By Meal / Component preserves printed ingredient detail, package size, ranges, preparation wording, serving notes, and optional ingredients.
- Crock Pot cards that intentionally name an ingredient without a quantity display “amount not specified.” No quantity was guessed.
- CR glaze ranges such as 2–3 tablespoons milk remain visible as printed.
- CR-001 through CR-004 now use the same titles as the existing Nutrition Database.

## Source-card review notes

Those values are preserved exactly because this phase records the cards rather than silently rewriting recipes. They should be reviewed as recipe-content corrections later.
