# v84.32 Secondary Inventory Strip Refinement

## Width and alignment

The Freezer and Pantry secondary strips now span 100% of their inner content area. Their left and right edges align with the filter row and full-width accordion edges indicated in the supplied markup.

## Vertical position

The secondary strip moves upward 30px on desktop and tablet, substantially reducing the blank area below the master command strip. Small screens use a 20px upward adjustment to preserve breathing room.

## Typography

Secondary segment labels increase to 14px with a stronger weight. The category name remains centered within each third of the strip.

## Count-circle position

Each number circle is independently positioned near the right edge of its segment. This moves the circle away from the category wording and matches the direction shown in the annotated example.
