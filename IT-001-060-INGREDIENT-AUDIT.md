# IT-001–IT-060 Ingredient Audit

Finalized September 4, 2026 from the cumulative v96.16 base.

## Applied standards

- Added all 60 Italian recipes and 646 ingredient rows to the site-wide five-part ingredient model.
- Preserved original recipe-card ingredient text while exposing standardized cooking and Shopping List fields.
- Converted small onions to 1/2 cup and unspecified medium onions to 1 cup.
- Converted package sizes to ounces and separated preparation instructions from ingredient identity.
- Excluded parsley, basil, and optional lemon-slice garnishes from automatic Shopping Lists.
- Added approved shopping weights for chicken pieces, fish fillets, prepared breaded cutlets, cooked sausage, and meatballs.
- Added approved cup equivalents for bell peppers, carrots, zucchini, yellow squash, eggplant, and Roma tomatoes.
- Converted 2 cups cooked chicken to about 1.5 pounds raw boneless chicken for shopping.

## Review status

All 31 previously flagged Italian ingredient rows now use the eight approved conversion rules. No unresolved ingredient conversions remain in IT-001–IT-060.

Recipe IDs, approved images, recipe-card designs, directions, and unrelated functionality remain unchanged.
