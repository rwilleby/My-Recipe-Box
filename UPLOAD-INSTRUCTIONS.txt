Hero-video behavior:
- Hero videos open and begin playing whenever the user enters an eligible page.
- The video window closes automatically after playback ends.
- Every hero-video window provides Play Now, Close Window, and Turn Off Auto Play.
- Close Window closes only the current window; it does not disable future auto-play.
- Turn Off Auto Play saves one site-wide preference. Afterward, hero videos open only when the user clicks a page's video button.
- Play Now restores sound when browser auto-play had to begin muted.

This package does not replace Meal Builder files or recipe-card hero assets.
