# HS-001–HS-020 Ingredient Audit

Approved September 5, 2026. Ingredient Standard version 1.35.

## Scope

- 20 Holiday Special recipes
- 163 ingredient rows restructured from manifest-authored display lines
- Original source text retained on every ingredient
- Recipe IDs, cards, hero images, directions, servings, and designs preserved

## Applied standards

- Quantities and units were extracted from all 163 source lines.
- Preparation instructions were separated from ingredient identity.
- Filet mignon retains four six-ounce steaks with a 24-ounce shopping total.
- The whole turkey and jalapeños retain their explicit source ranges.
- Small onions and single chopped or diced bell peppers use approved volume equivalents.
- Measured cheddar and butter receive practical shopping equivalents.
- Butter or olive oil remains an acceptable substitution, not two required purchases.
- Optional maple syrup remains visible but is excluded from automatic Shopping Lists.

## Retained review flags

- 9 prepared-produce rows omit a size needed for a defensible cup conversion.
- 1 canned-tomato row omits the can size.

No uncertain quantity was invented.
