# My Recipe Box v99.17

This release applies the approved library display standards across Complete Dinners, Healthy Dinners, Salad Jars, Crock Pot Meals, Browse Our Recipe Library, Browse Our Vegan Library, Your Favorites, and Holiday Featured Menus.

- Page-specific control strips now use the approved ordering, labels, and dropdown choices.
- The `ALL` segment is half the width of a standard category segment.
- Vegan recipes and Favorites use the standard three-column compact recipe cards.
- Vegan rotating recipes and numbered pagination are removed.
- Results counts use the standard `found · showing` presentation.
- Recipe lists load 12 at a time with the standard Show More button and remaining count.
- Holiday Featured Menus display one row of three compact recipe cards.
