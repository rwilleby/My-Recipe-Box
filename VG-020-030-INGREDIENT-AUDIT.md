# VG-020–VG-030 Ingredient Audit

Approved September 6, 2026. Ingredient Standard version 1.37.

## Scope

- 11 active Vegan recipes
- 99 ingredient rows
- Active-catalog completion: 773 recipes and 7,703 ingredient rows standardized
- Recipe IDs, images, directions, nutrition, and unrelated functionality preserved

## Applied standards

- Specific bean, chickpea, and jackfruit identities are retained for inventory matching.
- Whole produce and breads retain natural item counts.
- Cooked rice receives an approximate dry-rice shopping equivalent.
- Vegan product requirements remain attached to broth, BBQ sauce, breadcrumbs, buns, and pita.
- The invalid `set` cooking unit was removed from composite seasonings.

## Retained review flags

- 11 composite seasoning rows lack individual quantities and herb varieties.
- 8 canned-product rows lack can sizes.
- 4 grain or lentil rows do not specify dry versus cooked state.
- 3 ingredients do not specify the required product form.
- 1 cabbage-slaw row does not specify prepared mix versus individually prepared cabbage.

No uncertain quantity or product form was invented.
