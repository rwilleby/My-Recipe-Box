# Robert's Recipe Box v84.14 — Master Freezer Inventory

## Result

Kitchen Inventory now has a simpler public menu:

1. Master Kitchen Inventory
2. Master Freezer Inventory
3. Pantry Inventory

Refrigerator Inventory, Prepared Freezer Inventory, and the former separate Freezer Inventory are hidden from the menu. Their saved browser data and internal page compatibility remain intact; this update does not delete user inventory.

## Master Freezer Inventory

The former Freezer Inventory Management page is now Master Freezer Inventory with three equal-width controls:

- **Complete Meals** — tracks frozen complete dinners and package counts.
- **Individual Recipes** — tracks packaged entrées, sides, desserts, and other individual recipes.
- **Component Items** — tracks foods used to assemble meals, including bulk meats, cooked proteins, frozen vegetables and fruit, breads, sides, prepared foods, sauces, cooking bases, dairy extras, convenience foods, and freezer supplies.

Component Items uses the established freezer inventory records, so existing checked items, quantities, dates, locations, status, custom entries, and grocery-list decisions continue to work. Users may also add custom component items within any category.

## Retained tools

- Search, category, location, and stock-status filtering
- Quantity and package-size tracking
- Date frozen and use-by dates
- Multiple freezer locations and custom locations
- Running-low, use-soon, and grocery-list actions
- Custom component items
- Printed and digital stock checks
- Current-inventory printing
- JSON import and export
- Clear Inventory with confirmation
