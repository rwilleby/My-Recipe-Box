# v84.26 Matching Inventory Accordions

## Result

All three views within the unified Kitchen Inventory page now use the approved Kitchen Inventory accordion language: a warm taupe category header, Georgia category title, directional disclosure arrow, stocked/total count, white content area, matching border, and matching corner radius.

## Freezer organization

- Complete Meals are grouped by the cuisine/category of their main recipe.
- Individual Recipes are grouped by their recipe cuisine/category.
- Each accordion reports stocked meals or recipes versus the number available in that group.
- Component Items remain grouped by practical freezer-product categories because bulk meats, vegetables, breads, sauces, and cooking components do not always have a cuisine.
- Component Item headers now use the same visual treatment as Kitchen Inventory.

## Pantry organization

- Each visible pantry category is one accordion.
- Each header reports items marked In Stock versus the number displayed at the selected pantry level.
- Custom Pantry Items receive their own accordion when present.

## Behavior

- Freezer and Pantry accordions start closed.
- Entering a current-section search automatically opens matching accordion groups.
- Changing between Complete Meals, Individual Recipes, and Component Items resets the Freezer groups to closed.
- The v84.25 master Inventory Control Strip was intentionally left unchanged.
