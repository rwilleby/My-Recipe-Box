RRB POCKET PANTRY — ADD-ON FOR THE SUPPLIED v100.3 ZIP
September 30, 2026

INSTALL ON YOUR WEBSITE
1. Extract this add-on ZIP.
2. Copy its files into the matching folders of your current RRB project, replacing matching files. Keep the rest of your project.
3. Commit the changed files to your GitHub repository. The existing GitHub Actions workflow installs dependencies and builds the website. Wait for its green checkmark.
   For a local build, run npm ci, then npm run build.
4. The new page is /pantry-capture.html on your RRB domain.

ADD TO YOUR IPHONE
Open that page in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Name it RRB Pantry. This is an iPhone-friendly web app; it is not an App Store download. An internet connection is needed to open it and load the local recognition tools. No account is required.

CAPTURE ITEMS
- Type or dictate product names, one per line, then Add to Review List.
- Take or choose a close photo of a product label or receipt, then tap Read Text. Correct the text before adding items. Nutrition lines, prices and other label details may need removing; a whole shelf photo is a reference, not reliable automatic product identification.
- Photograph a complete barcode straight on. RRB reads the barcode number. Enter the product name for a first-time barcode; previously saved products on that device can be recognized. This version does not query an online product database.
- Review the name, storage location, quantity, unit and notes.
- Set counted quantity replaces a matching product's count. Add purchased quantity adds to it. Exact name or barcode, unit and location must match; otherwise RRB creates a new product. No unit conversion or fuzzy automatic match is attempted.

SAVE OR TRANSFER
Add Reviewed Items to Inventory saves in the main Kitchen Inventory on this device and clears the review list.
To transfer to your Mac, use Share or Download Transfer File BEFORE saving. Use AirDrop or Save to Files.
On your Mac: RRB > Kitchen Inventory > Photo List > Open Transfer File. Review the items, then Add Reviewed Items to Inventory.
The small JSON transfer imports inventory items only. It does not replace your full Recipe Box backup. Already imported item IDs are skipped on repeated import.
The review list is saved locally across reloads. Photos are temporary, processed on your device and excluded from transfers. No automatic cross-device synchronization occurs.
Items are stored in main Kitchen Inventory, including Pantry, Refrigerator and Kitchen freezer locations. They are not separate entries in the older Pantry checkbox or Freezer tables.
Your standard RRB Backup & Restore includes both the saved main inventory and the pending review list through its existing rrb_ storage coverage.
