# v84.34 Beige Inventory Controls

## Pantry

The Pantry summary, action borders, and selected action fills now use warm beige and taupe tones instead of green. The Fully Stocked Pantry summary centers its title, description, and shelf-stable note.

## Kitchen

The three summary counters and the stocked/forms figures in accordion headers are hidden without deleting or changing inventory data. Kitchen controls now use the same warm neutral palette.

The command strip contains exactly six actions in one row:

`MANUAL INVENTORY | EXPAND | COLLAPSE | ADD ITEM | PURCHASE | CLEAR`

The actions retain their existing behavior: worksheet printing, accordion expansion/collapse, custom-item entry, purchase recording, and clearing Buy quantities.
