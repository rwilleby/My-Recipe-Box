# v84.29 Inventory Layout and Sorting

## Result

The unified inventory interface now uses the full available page width for its master command strip and every Kitchen, Freezer, and Pantry accordion.

## Navigation

Freezing & Reheating now appears in **Kitchen Details**, immediately beneath **Master Kitchen Inventory**. It was removed from **Your Kitchen**.

## Alphabetical organization

- Kitchen master-category accordions are alphabetical; product families and their variations are alphabetical inside each category.
- Freezer cuisine accordions are alphabetical; Complete Meals and Individual Recipes are alphabetical within each cuisine.
- Freezer Component Item categories and their products are alphabetical.
- Pantry category accordions and all visible products inside them are alphabetical.

Sorting happens only in the displayed view. Stored inventory records and stable item identifiers are unchanged.

## Pantry and Freezer controls

- The Pantry stock-status selector is 110px wide so Not Set, In Stock, Low, and Out remain fully visible.
- Complete Meals and Individual Recipes now use the label **Servings**.
- The Frozen Packages and Servings controls share matching label and input rows for vertical alignment.
