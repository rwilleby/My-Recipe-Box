# Robert's Recipe Box v84.16 Kitchen Details Report

## Main-menu naming

The public menu now reads:

1. Recipes & Meals
2. Kitchen Details
   - Kitchen Inventory
   - Freezer Inventory
   - Pantry Inventory

Internal page identifiers and browser-storage keys remain unchanged so bookmarks, navigation wiring, and saved user data continue to work.

## Kitchen Inventory

- Every category accordion starts closed.
- Each product family uses a two-line dark header band. The family name occupies the complete first line without wrapping; Storage, Form, Cut / Variety, Unit, Have, Buy, and Notes appear on the second line.
- Storage is wider so Refrigerator remains visible. The Form column begins farther right.
- The redundant “Recipe” label is removed from Cut / Variety.
- Recipe-derived names are grouped into stable families instead of becoming separate headings.
- Ground Beef has seven consolidated forms: 80/20 raw, 90/10 raw, 90/10 cooked, 93% lean raw, 97/3 raw, lean raw, and lean cooked.
- Chicken has eleven consolidated forms covering raw breasts, thighs, drumsticks, wings, quarters, whole chicken, and cooked chicken breast. Cooked whole, sliced, diced, and shredded chicken breast are tracked by cups.
- Legacy recipe-derived record keys are retained as aliases, allowing previously saved quantities to remain visible under the new grouped form.

## Shared page-top structure

Kitchen Inventory and Pantry Inventory now use the established centered 25px Georgia title, centered 14px supporting copy, three summary cards, and rounded control-strip styling. Pantry levels use the approved muted gray/taupe segmented-navigation treatment. The control-strip contents can be refined later without rebuilding the page structure.
