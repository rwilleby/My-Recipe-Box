## Ingredient handling

- By Meal / Component preserves printed ingredient detail, package size, preparation wording, alternatives, and optional status.
- Combined salt-and-pepper lines are separate grocery checkboxes so either pantry item can be marked independently.
- Unmeasured garnishes and serving items retain “amount not specified” instead of receiving invented quantities.
- True alternatives such as “cilantro or green onions” and “salsa or sour cream” remain one choice so the Shopping List does not demand both.
- LF-FZ1 and LF-FZ2 are freezer-instruction cards, so they intentionally generate no purchases.
- All seven KR titles and MX-043 now use the same names as the existing Nutrition Database.

## Source-card review notes

- MX-008 directions mention 1/4 cup water, but its printed ingredient panel does not. Water was not added because the project rule is to transcribe the ingredient panel without inference.
- MX-034's avocado, cilantro, and lime serving items are separate checkboxes because each is an independent inventory decision.
- Exact source values retained include JJ-008's 12 ounces of bacon, JJ-014's 1/2 cup granulated Splenda, LF-015's 1 3/4 teaspoons salt, MX-012's 1/3 cup milk, and MX-035's 1/4 teaspoon salt and pepper.
