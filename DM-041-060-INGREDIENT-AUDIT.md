# DM-041–DM-060 Ingredient Audit

Approved September 5, 2026. Ingredient Standard version 1.34.

## Scope

- 20 Diet Meals recipes
- 201 ingredient rows
- No source transcription corrections required
- Recipe IDs, imagery, directions, nutrition, card designs, and unrelated functionality preserved

## Applied standards

- Meat and seafood retain ounce cooking quantities; 16 ounces receives a one-pound shopping equivalent.
- Cod or tilapia remains an acceptable substitution group.
- Cooked rice retains cooked cup measures with approximate dry-rice shopping equivalents.
- Chopped red bell peppers use one cup with an approximate one-pepper shopping equivalent.
- Measured cheddar, pepper Jack, Parmesan, and feta receive approximate ounce equivalents.
- Preparation text is separated from recipe-facing and canonical ingredient identities.

## Retained review flags

- 4 black-pepper rows have no measurable source quantity.
- 4 composite-produce rows do not give individual proportions.
- 3 chopped whole vegetables do not provide a size or defensible cup yield.
- Cauliflower-rice package yield varies by selected product.
- Roasted sweet potatoes do not provide a raw weight or potato size.
- Cooked whole-wheat couscous does not provide a defensible product-independent dry conversion.

No uncertain amount was invented.
