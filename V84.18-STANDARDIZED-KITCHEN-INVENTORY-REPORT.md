# Robert's Recipe Box v84.18 Standardized Kitchen Inventory Report

## Controlled taxonomy

Kitchen Inventory now uses the supplied 11 master categories and 248 approved product types as its source of truth. Every visible catalog row must resolve to an approved product type. The former Other Recipe Ingredients category and separate Spices, Seasonings & Baking category are no longer visible.

The Sauces, Condiments & Baking taxonomy retains the four supplied internal groups: Sauces, Condiments, Baking, and Seasonings.

## Canonical identity

The inventory identity is:

`Master Category → Product Type → Cut / Variety → Preparation / Form → Brand`

Storage, Unit, Have, Buy, and Notes remain operational tracking fields. Brand is editable per household record, allowing a recipe to remain brand-neutral while the user records a preferred or currently stocked brand.

The generated catalog deduplicates on category, product type, cut/variety, and preparation/form. Counting-unit differences cannot create a second product identity.

## Storage-form routing

- Canned foods move to Canned & Jarred Foods.
- Frozen foods move to Frozen Foods.
- Instant potatoes move to Prepared & Packaged Foods.
- Tortillas, wraps, buns, and related bread products move to Bread & Bakery.
- Seafood is separated from mixed “Meat & Seafood” aisle metadata.
- Unmatched source wording resolves to an approved category-specific fallback product instead of creating a new heading.

## Chicken example

Chicken uses Breast as the Cut / Variety. Diced, Sliced, Shredded, Whole, Raw, and Cooked are stored in Preparation / Form. This produces entries such as:

`Meat & Poultry → Chicken → Breast → Diced, Raw → Tyson`

The brand is entered by the user and is not imposed on the recipe card.

## Saved-data protection

Existing curated IDs, v84.16 generated IDs, v84.17 generated IDs, and source-name aliases are retained during merging. Previously saved Have, Buy, Storage, Notes, and additional-location records continue to resolve under the new headings.
