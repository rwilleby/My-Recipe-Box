# Robert's Recipe Box v83.6 Navigation Report

## Main-menu order

The main navigation now appears in this order beneath the logo:

1. ABOUT US
2. KITCHEN INVENTORY
3. RECIPES & MEALS
4. MEAL PLANNING
5. SHOPPING
6. RESOURCES

The existing Favorites heart shortcut remains at the end of the navigation row.

## Kitchen Inventory

The new KITCHEN INVENTORY section contains these existing destinations in the requested order:

1. MASTER KITCHEN INVENTORY
2. REFRIGERATOR INVENTORY
3. PREPARED FREEZER INVENTORY
4. FREEZER INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
5. FREEZER INVENTORY
6. PANTRY INVENTORY

All six are non-indented submenu entries. Their page routes, Detailed-mode visibility rules, and video-availability indicators remain unchanged.

## Recipes & Meals

The former RECIPES heading is now RECIPES & MEALS. YOUR FAVORITE RECIPES appears immediately after BROWSE OUR RECIPE LIBRARY and before DINNER COMBINATIONS. It has been removed from MEAL PLANNING.

## Responsive fit

Only the spacing and responsive font range of the top-level navigation were reduced. Submenu sizing and the approved below-logo structure remain unchanged. The iPad click/tap, outside-tap closing, and Escape-key behavior remain active.
