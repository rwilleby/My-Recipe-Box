# CP-121–CP-140 Ingredient Audit

- Category: Crock Pot Meals
- Recipes audited: 20
- Ingredient rows audited: 171
- Ingredient standard: 1.29
- Transcription corrections: 16
- Ingredient-row review flags retained: 55
- Recipe-level review flags retained: 1

## Approved treatments

- Protein identities and preparations remain intact; whole-count pork tenderloins are flagged because their total weight is absent.
- Prepared carrots, celery, grated apple, and sliced scallions use volume measures and practical whole-produce equivalents.
- Cheese shopping equivalents use approximate ounces, eggs and jumbo shells use natural item counts, and dry grain or pasta state is explicit.
- Generic broth and cream soup remain generic. Optional unmeasured cheese remains visible and excluded from the Shopping List.
- Slurry water specified only in directions remains a cooking supply rather than an invented ingredient row.

## Review flags

Ten package rows omit sizes, forty-three rows omit quantities, one pork-tenderloin row omits total weight, and one sun-dried-tomato row omits product form. CP-125 is separately flagged because its directions require eggplant while its source ingredient list supplies no eggplant quantity.
