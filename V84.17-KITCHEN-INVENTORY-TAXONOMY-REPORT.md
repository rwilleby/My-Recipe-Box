# Robert's Recipe Box v84.17 Kitchen Inventory Taxonomy Report

## Result

The Kitchen Inventory Meat & Poultry accordion now uses one controlled taxonomy. All recipe-card meat terms are normalized into the requested master family, a Form value, and a Cut / Variety value. The recipe cards themselves retain natural cooking language.

## Master family order

1. Bacon
2. Beef
3. Ground Beef
4. Chicken
5. Ground Chicken
6. Pork
7. Ground Pork
8. Turkey
9. Ground Turkey
10. Ham
11. Sausages
12. Smoking Meats
13. Deli

## Chicken structure

Raw and Cooked appear in Form. Cut / Variety contains Breast, Thigh, Bone-In Thigh, Drumstick, Wing, Leg Quarter, Whole Chicken, Whole Breasts, Diced Breast, Sliced Breast, and Shredded Breast. Cooked breast forms use cups so inventory can combine cooked chicken by volume.

## Compatibility

Existing v84.16 curated record IDs are retained where labels changed. Recipe-derived source names are also retained as aliases. This allows saved Have, Buy, Storage, and Notes data to continue resolving under the new visible labels.

## Unit column

The desktop Unit column minimum increased from 58px to 128px. Tablet and mobile layouts also receive wider Unit columns. Unit text can wrap, preventing longer values from being ellipsized.
