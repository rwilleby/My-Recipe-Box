## Ingredient handling

- By Meal / Component shows every distinct ingredient printed on each source card.
- Printed quantities, ranges, package sizes, preparation wording, true alternatives, optional status, garnishes, and serving items are retained.
- Unmeasured items use an explicit shopping unit such as “amount not specified,” “to taste,” or “for serving”; no quantity is guessed.
- Independent salt and pepper needs remain separate checkboxes. True alternatives remain one choice.
- QP-021 prints salt twice; the shopping data intentionally provides one salt checkbox rather than a duplicate purchase line.
- SG-022 mentions olive oil only in its directions, not its ingredient panel, so oil was not inferred.

## Identity review

Recipe display titles were aligned with the existing Nutrition Database where the identities agree. SG-012 is a documented source-data exception: its card and recipe are **Smoked Pork Ribs**, while the Nutrition Database row for SG-012 says **Lemon Pepper Chicken**. v84.15 deliberately leaves the rib identity intact and does not silently attach chicken nutrition to it. The nutrition source row needs a separate correction when authoritative rib nutrition is available.
